THE ROOM BETWEEN LIVES
A novel in four books
————
Wren Calloway hasn't spoken in six years — not since the night in her kitchen that ended with a gunshot, her sister's name on her lips, and a silence the tabloids turned into a story that was never actually true. While her body lies in a hospital bed the doctors can't explain, her mind checks into the Between: a hotel that exists in no place at all, run by a concierge named Aldous, with one door for every life she almost lived.
Behind those doors, she becomes someone else, completely, for as long as each life takes to finish: a hotel maid whose gift for noticing solves a guest's murder. A research chemist who gets her work stolen — and takes it back live on national television. Two childhood friends who spend forty years building worlds together, until grief nearly breaks what they built, and finishing a dead friend's unfinished work brings them home. An astronaut who wakes six years into a one-way mission to discover the thing she was sent to destroy is dying right alongside the world she's trying to save. A cadet who trusts the one creature everyone else called worthless — and it saves her life. A ghostwriter who spent fourteen years disappearing into other people's names, until she decides to disappear no longer.
Every door teaches her the same lesson from a different angle: notice what's真actually in front of you, say the true thing even when it costs you, finish what's unfinished, choose hope on purpose, trust what's earned it, take your own name back.
There's one door left — the one with no plaque, at the end of the hall, that isn't optional. Behind it is the truth about her sister, the eleven months Wren refused to believe her, and the single word she said before she stopped speaking at all.
She has to open it before she can wake up. And she has to wake up before she can finally say what happened.
————
A four-part psychological drama that moves between a locked-room mystery, a scientist's reckoning, a forty-year friendship, a space rescue, a war academy, and a publishing scandal — all in service of one woman's silence, and what it finally takes to break it.
